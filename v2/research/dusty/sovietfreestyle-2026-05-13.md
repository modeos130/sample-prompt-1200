## Prompt Territories

1. Moldavian State Disco-Funk
   - Source basis: Melodiya 1983 Soviet/Moldavia listing with disco, funk, loop, and hip-hop sample language.
   - Creative inference: Chisinau state-studio session, electric piano, analog synthesizer, electric bass, string machine.
   - Emotional color: guarded optimism under state-studio polish.
   - Scarcity frame: domestic Soviet pressing, rarely exported.
   - Prompt Lab genre fit: soviet-disco-funk or eastern-bloc-synth-funk.

2. Armenian Scat Jazz-Funk

   - Emotional color: bright on the surface, uneasy underneath.
   - Scarcity frame: regional Soviet republic pressing.
   - Prompt Lab genre fit: caucasus-jazz-funk.

3. Late Soviet Eerie Synth Rock
   - Source basis: 1988 Melodiya listing tagged dark, eerie, synth, rock, trippy.
   - Creative inference: late-Soviet studio, analog polysynth, chorus guitar, fretless bass, cold plate reverb.
   - Emotional color: fluorescent anxiety, end-of-era unease.
   - Scarcity frame: domestic late-Soviet pressing with limited export.
   - Prompt Lab genre fit: late-soviet-synth-drama.

4. Soviet OST Moog Funk
   - Source basis: Russian/Melodiya OST listings tagged synth, disco-funk, Moog, breaks, samples.

   - Scarcity frame: soundtrack cue from domestic cinema distribution.
